## How to play

1. Open [@rivalrushbot](https://t.me/rivalrushbot) and tap **Start**, then **Play** (or the
   **Play** button next to the message box).
2. You're signed in automatically with your Telegram account.
3. **Start a duel** → choose the settings (code length 3–5 digits, 30–90 s per turn, 8–12
   guesses) → **Create room**.
4. **Send invite** opens Telegram's chat picker. Your friend taps the link and **Join game**.
5. Your friend taps **I'm ready**, you tap **Start game**.
6. Both of you have 60 s to hide a code (different digits, no repeats). If time runs out, the
   game picks one for you.
7. Take turns guessing. Each guess shows bulls (right place) and cows (wrong place).
8. If the first player cracks the code, the second player gets **one last chance** to tie.
9. After the result: **Rematch** (the other player goes first) or **Leave**.

Your profile shows wins, losses, draws, win rate, streaks and your recent games.

## Good to know

- **Updates end live games.** When a fix is deployed, games in progress stop and you'll see
  "This room is gone". Start a new duel; finished games and stats are safe.
- **Dropping out:** if you lose connection, you have 60 seconds to come back. After that the
  game counts as a loss ("Dropped out"). Switching apps for a few seconds is fine.
- **Giving up** counts as a loss.
- **The first open of the day can be slow** (up to a minute) if the server was asleep.
- If the app ever says your session is too old, close it and open it again from the bot.
- **Invite links** stop working when everyone has left the room, after 2 hours idle, or after an
  update.
  Create a new room.

## Reporting a problem

Post in the beta group or message the organiser. Please include:

1. **What you were doing** (for example: "my turn, I tapped Guess").
2. **What happened**, and what you expected instead.
3. **A screenshot** or screen recording, if you can.
4. **Your phone and Telegram app:** iPhone or Android, and whether Telegram is up to date.
5. **When it happened** (time and your time zone).
6. **Did it recover** after closing and reopening the app?

For a wrong result or wrong bulls/cows, a screenshot of the result screen (it shows both
codes) and of the guess list is the most useful thing you can send.

## What we store

Your Telegram id, name, username, profile photo link and language, plus your game history
(guesses, scores and results) and stats. Secret codes are never stored. RivalRush cannot see
your chats, contacts or phone number.
